ORBITSCORE / WIRING & ASSEMBLY / REV A
Disconnect USB before installing parts or changing any harness.

PICO PIN MAP (GP identifiers are not physical header pin numbers)
GP2kick, GP3snare, GP4hat, GP5tom, GP6clock, GP7index: outputs of HC14.
GP8..GP11: mute buttons, active-low to ground, internal pulls enabled.
GP12scan button; GP13play/cancel button: active-low to ground.
GP14scan LED, GP15play LED, each through1k to anode; cathode grounded.
GP16I2S BCLK; GP17I2S LRCLK; GP18I2S data; GP19amplifier shutdown.
GP26tempo and GP27volume: wipers of10k linear panel potentiometers.
Pot outside terminals:3.3V and GND. Reverse those two to reverse knob direction.
Pico physical pin36 supplies3.3V; physical pin40 is USB VBUS5V.
The Pico socket uses2x20 rows,17.78mm between rows and2.54mm pitch.
Orient the Pico USB connector toward the top of the controller board.

J1 OPTICAL HARNESS: connect numbered pins one-to-one, not by cable color.
Main board connector is1x8, reader connector is2x4. Make an explicit harness:
1=3V3,2=GND,3=raw kick,4=raw snare,5=raw hat,6=raw tom,7=raw clock,8=raw index.
On the reader2x4 footprint, pad1 is the square pad; pins1/2 share a row,
then3/4,5/6,7/8. Read the numbered pads in KiCad before soldering.
A straight IDC ribbon is not appropriate for this mixed connector geometry.
Keep cable short (target<150mm), ground beside the sensor bundle if possible.

J2 EMITTER HARNESS
1=3V3,2=GND on both ends. Never connect5V to this header.
Emitter LEDs face downward; phototransistors face upward.
For custom IR footprints: pad1=cathode, pad2=anode.
For custom BPW85C footprints: pad1=emitter, pad2=collector.
Verify the vendor lead identification against its drawing before trimming.
The rectangular custom bodies are drawn on the fabrication layer, not the silk,
to keep the solder-mask openings clean. Assembly SVG exports show body outlines.

J3 AUDIO AMPLIFIER HARNESS
Header1=LRCLK,2=DIN,3=BCLK,4=GAIN(open),5=SD,6=GND,7=5V.
Connect by signal name to the Adafruit MAX98357A module. This connector is for
an external cable, not a claim that every compatible amplifier shares pin order.
Use the module's supplied speaker screw terminal for the loudspeaker.
Do not connect a headphone jack or grounded oscilloscope clip to a speaker output.
Mount the amplifier insulated from the enclosure fasteners. Leave GAIN open.

CONTROLLER COMPONENTS
U2 is DIP14 HC14, not HCT14 (HCT has a different supply range).
Pin14=3V3; pin7=GND. Gate input/output pairs:1/2,3/4,5/6,9/8,11/10,13/12.
Install C1 close to U2. C1/C2 are100nF decouplers. C3/C4 are10nF ADC filters.
C5 is470uF,10V minimum, positive to5V and negative stripe toGND.
R1=100k shutdown pull-down. R2=10k RUN pull-up. R3/R4=1k LED resistors.
Six6mm tactile switches use6.5x4.5mm four-leg pad geometry. Confirm supplied
switch drawing before ordering. Two same-numbered pads are the same contact.

MECHANICAL ASSEMBLY
Print parts flat at0.2mm layer height. Use PETG or PLA with opaque dark material
for the optical bridge. Print a small hole/shaft tolerance coupon first.
Base and deck:190mm square, M3 corner centers(7,7),(183,7),(7,183),(183,183).
Speaker mounting reference:60mm square, centers(35,110),(95,110),(35,170),(95,170).
Use compliant washers and verify the speaker frame against its actual drawing.
Reader board origin in base coordinates:(96,84), bottom surface z27.4mm.
Its lens centers are at x116,126,136,146,156,166 and y90.
Disc center:(90,90); deck bottom z31, top33.4; hub flange top37.4.
Disc runs fromz37.4 to38.2. Check no sensor tip reaches that plane.
Emitter board is above the disc, components downward; flip it about its x-axis.
Its optics align with the reader's six lens centers. Target board bottomz48.6.
Bridge attaches outside the disc path and is removable for changing discs.
Use approximately0.8mm insulating washers to set emitter-board height beneath
the bridge. Confirm actual LED package height; preserve at least1mm disc clearance.
Reader/emitter bar mounting hole centers are local(3,9),(86,9).
Controller PCB mounts inside the pod with its origin offset(5,5), bottomz7.
Pot holes in lid are at(55,18),(80,18); wire them to J4 and J5.
Six printed plungers reach the short tactile stems through the lid openings.
Fit and trim plungers to switch height so none is pressed at rest.
Pico USB access is an oversized cable opening; use a short flexible cable and
check connector reach before fastening the lid. No fit is physically verified.
