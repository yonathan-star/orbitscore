ORBITSCORE / BENCH BRING-UP & ACCEPTANCE PLAN
These are future checks, not completed measurements.
1. Inspect pad polarity, DIP notch, solder bridges, and electrolytic polarity.
2. With modules removed and USB disconnected, check3V3/GND and5V/GND for shorts.
3. Power the Pico alone from a current-limited5V source. Confirm3.3V regulator output.
4. Add HC14 and the reader. Measure dark collector voltage>2.8V and illuminated
   collector voltage<0.5V on every channel. HC14 output should invert these.
5. Start with one IR LED/resistor and verify current7-10mA, then add the other five.
6. Place opaque material between pairs. Verify every signal changes independently
   without adjacent-channel leakage. Test room lamps and window light with hood.
7. Flash official MicroPython1.26 RP2 firmware appropriate for the original Pico.
   Copy core.py,synth.py,main.py into board root. Capture serial error messages.
8. Disconnect speaker, check audio clocks16kHz LRCLK and the expected data stream.
   Add amplifier and speaker. Start volume at zero, then raise slowly.
9. Time one hundred128-frame rendering/writing cycles. Check sustained audio
   without underruns, late controls, or excessive garbage-collection pauses.
   Host unit tests do not verify this Pico performance.
10. Scan the supplied disc clockwise at2,4,8 seconds/revolution. Compare the printed
    bit masks against pattern.json. Scan again with one intentionally missing
    clock hole: it must reject the new scan and preserve the old pattern.
11. Play at60,120,180BPM. Verify loop lengths8,4,2.667sec respectively for TWO bars;
    one16-step bar should be4,2,1.333sec. Record measured results, not estimates.
12. Test every mute, cancel, knob endpoint, restart, and power-cycle persistence.
13. Inspect printed fit, shaft play, disc flatness, and optical window alignment.
    Print adjustments before calling the design fabrication-ready.
14. Measure USB current at maximum intended volume and record a10-minute run.

EXPECTED STARTUP BEHAVIOR
No default demo beat is automatically substituted for a failed scan.
A missing pattern file starts silent. Scan timeout is30sec. Play cancels a scan.
The previous stored pattern survives incomplete scans. A pattern is saved only
while stopped. Four voices are mixed with bounded sample levels.

TEST EVIDENCE TO RECORD
Actual circuit photos, optical voltage/current readings, capture comparison,
Pico software version, rendering time, audio recording, and enclosure fit photos.
